Overview

The problem

CS reps spent days preparing just to decide which customers needed outreach, and in what order. Churn risk often hid in conversations other reps had with the account, even while the usage data looked healthy. And after every connected call, reps lost hours to the follow-up, because not every customer question could be answered live and most issues couldn't be resolved on the spot.

The approach

The Success Assistant already let reps ask the AI anything and get a useful answer, but it was reactive; it waited to be asked. This vision makes Customer Success proactive: a system that catches risk and opportunity before a rep would, prepares the response as reviewable drafts, and leaves every decision with the rep. It works as one arc:

1Prioritize the book

The Orchestrator is the proactive engine, not an agent reps talk to. It continuously reads the context layer and the rep's whole book for the two signals humans miss: accounts sliding toward churn while the usage still looks healthy, and accounts ready to grow. It ranks who needs the rep today, shows why, and sets the downstream agents to work.

2Reach out

The Outreach agent drafts proactive, personalized outreach in the rep's voice, timed to that account's moment. The rep reviews, adjusts, and sends. The agent writes; the rep decides.

3Meet and follow-up

The Meeting agent drafts the prep before the call and the recap after, while the Live call assistant surfaces answers in the moment. Prep, live call, and recap flow as one experience, each a draft the rep confirms, and the recap feeds the next cycle so the system sharpens over time.

Discovery research

I built the vision on the following three layers of evidence:

Synthesized existing research

  • I pulled every relevant past study, mine and other designers', into NotebookLM to surface recurring pain-point themes and map the rep's journeys.
  • These journeys uncovered where automation will be useful.
  • NotebookLM let cross-functional stakeholders ask deeper questions and validate the findings and direction.

Mined the Success Assistant's chat logs

I had designed HubSpot CS's first conversational AI assistant before this vision. Using Claude, I analyzed its real chat logs against the pain themes, cross-matching to see what reps were actually trying to accomplish with proprietary HubSpot data behind the assistant.

Behavioural log analysis

Validated with CS reps

I interviewed CS reps of varying tenures and geo-locations to pressure-test the themes and pinpoint where the real gaps were, grounding the vision in their reality.

In-depth interviews

Reps were spending days deciding who to call, right as the bar for connected calls got higher.

The core insight

Reps spent a huge share of their time just prioritizing their book of business, manually combining:

  • Unified Usage Score (a health model I had designed earlier)
  • Past conversations, literally reading through engagements (emails, call logs, notes)
  • Subscription data
  • Product updates that fit a particular customer need

Success Assistant helped prioritize, draft outreach and prep a lot faster than before, but it still required reps to prompt it well. At that moment, most reps weren't AI fluent, and most of what they were trying to do could be automated and performed upfront for them to review.

Designing the system end-to-end

A completely redesigned CS Workspace, where reps begin their day without piecing together what to do next.

  • Top priorities are surfaced, ranked, and ready to go: no digging, no second-guessing.
  • AI-prepared strategies, emails, and plans are waiting at their fingertips.
  • Calendar, goals, and metrics all in one view, seamlessly connected.
  • With the Success Assistant built in, the rep doesn't work alone. One click to shift focus, refine priorities, or ask it to spotlight something new.
Orchestrator agent

Actions focused view – where things get done

  • This is where the rep moves from one high-impact task to the next, seamlessly.
  • Whatever the task - a follow-up, a save play, or a key email - it's already teed up and ready for them to review and confirm. No digging, no switching tools.
  • And if they need more context or want to tweak something, the Success Assistant is right there on the side - not flooding them with information, just offering exactly what they need, when they need it.
  • With the Success Assistant built in, the rep doesn't work alone. One click to shift focus, refine priorities, or ask it to spotlight something new.

From scattered meeting prep to confident conversations.

  • The Orchestrator triggers the Meeting Agent upon reading the rep's calendar and creates prep material for upcoming customer calls.
  • The agent performs a comprehensive research of the customer analyzing usage trends, business goals, renewal info, and past engagements. This research used to take days before AI.
  • Success Assistant, fully aware of the meeting context, helps the rep with any question they have about the customer — all without leaving their prep view.
  • When they highlight a detail, a quick prompt pop-up lets them ask deeper questions in place, making every insight just a click away.

Multiple agents can get triggered for one customer portal. The rep sees a streamlined sequence of agent outputs to review and complete the action.

In this example, the system grouped a set of actions into one guided sequence - so the rep can review a tailored growth plan and send it, all in one place.

Demo over memo – with vibecoded prototypes

I used Lovable for early concept prototyping. Later I also utilized Claude as we shipped the vision into production. At every stage, I shared Loom walkthroughs of the prototypes to align the teams and stakeholders.
